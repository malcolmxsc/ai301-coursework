# Rubric: is this reproduction package ready to post?

## Checks

| Check | Evidence | Pass condition | Weight |
|---|---|---|---|
| Specific, bounded claim | Candidate claim comment read against the issue's trigger, symptom, and thread context; evidence guide: Comms | The claim names a concrete detail of this issue and a concrete investigation or verification step the author intends to take. It does not promise a guaranteed fix or delivery date, demand assignment, or substitute an interchangeable “same here” for intent. An author who has already tested may describe that work if the accompanying report supports it. | required |
| Environment recorded | Repro report's environment record, version commands, installation/build details, and stated differences from the issue's target; evidence guide: Environment | The report identifies the tested software version or commit and operating system, plus relevant runtime, configuration, installation method, or build profile needed to place this attempt. Material differences from the reporter's setup are acknowledged and conclusions stay within the tested setup. Do not infer the author's environment from the issue's own environment or from an error message alone. | required |
| Steps a stranger can run | Repro report's setup, input/configuration, commands or UI actions, and trigger, read against the issue's sequence; evidence guide: Steps | Starting from the stated environment, another person can construct the necessary state and execute the attempt without guessing essential commands, inputs, flags, timing, or private data. Required minimal inputs and configuration are supplied or publicly accessible; an unambiguous prose construction recipe is sufficient when omitted values do not affect the trigger. Followability requires reaching the decisive symptom, not reproducing incidental success-message wording or unrelated dependency choices byte for byte. Steps exercise the issue's trigger, or explicitly describe a limited attempt and why it may not trigger the bug. | required |
| Evidence addresses the issue | Repro report's actual output, logs, screenshots, measurements, or other inspectable artifacts compared with the issue's distinctive behavior; evidence guide: Behavior shown | At least one artifact shows what happened during the stated attempt and distinguishes the issue's behavior from an adjacent failure. For a claimed reproduction, the artifact demonstrates the reported symptom with its relevant trigger; merely showing that the program runs or that some error occurred is insufficient. For cannot-reproduce, artifacts show the attempted trigger and observed non-failure, with limitations stated. | required |
| Outcome follows the evidence | Claim and repro conclusions, expected versus actual behavior, and causal assertions read against the artifacts and issue; evidence guide: Honesty | The report states whether the issue reproduced, did not reproduce, or remains inconclusive in the tested setup, and its conclusions agree with the shown behavior. It distinguishes observations from hypotheses and does not assert a cause, universality, crash, or successful verification beyond the evidence. An evidenced cannot-reproduce passes; confidently confirming a different failure does not. | required |
| Repository conventions | Claim and repro comments compared with the repo-facts bug-report requirements and contribution/AI policy, or live repository docs; evidence guide: Comms | The package follows the repository’s stated rules for this kind of comment. Follow-up repro comments may satisfy template asks with equivalent substantive information; do not require a new title, duplicate search, or full diagnostic dump when the report already supplies the relevant environment and proof, unless the policy explicitly requires that exact output for comments. Where disclosure of AI assistance is required, the comments explicitly disclose it; treat course eval packages and our own AI-assisted drafts as assisted work. Assistance alone does not prove that wording violates a human-authorship rule: assess the actual comment and stated provenance, and do not infer an unstated disclosure requirement. A policy limiting AI-generated code or PRs does not automatically prohibit a human-reviewed issue report. | required |

## Verdict rule

Accept only when every applicable required check passes. A required `fail` or `unclear` means reject; missing evidence is not a pass. Preferred checks, if added, never change the verdict. Grade substance, not length, formatting, or number of headings.

In live claim-only mode, apply Specific, bounded claim and Repository conventions to the claim's applicable requirements. Mark the four report-dependent checks `unclear` with evidence `not yet applicable: claim-only draft` and exclude them from the verdict. Do not demand report-only template fields before the investigation. In a full package, all checks apply. Eval mode always grades a full package using only its frozen contents; it does not apply my personal voice rules or infer a failed check from another student's claim.
